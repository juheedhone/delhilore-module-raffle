Stories from Delhi

Explore the hidden gems, forgotten poetry, and vibrant culture of the city.

The Whispering Walls

By Ayesha | May 2025

In a quiet alley of Old Delhi, there's a mural that changes with the seasons. Locals say that it tells the stories of poets whose verses were lost to time. Every brushstroke holds emotion, and when the wind blows a certain way, people swear they can hear the walls whispering forgotten rhymes. This living artwork captures the ever-changing soul of the city.

Tags: Street Art, History

Midnight Chai

By Rohan | April 2025

Hidden in plain sight, Delhi's secret midnight tea stalls offer more than a warm cup — they serve untold stories. At midnight, storytellers, rickshaw drivers, and night workers gather over chai brewed on ancient coal stoves. The aroma is thick with cardamom and nostalgia. Rohan's poem captures the haunting beauty of these moments — a ritual of silence and words shared between sips.

Tags: Poem, Culture

The Forgotten Stepwell

By Priya | March 2025

Just behind the chaos of Chandni Chowk lies a hidden stepwell, wrapped in silence and stone. Its carvings speak of ancient rituals, and moss lines the edges where children once played. Priya's photo essay reveals layers of Delhi that most have never seen — a time capsule of architecture, faith, and forgotten gatherings.

Tags: Photo, Heritage

Colors of Chandni Chowk

By Meera | February 2025

Every wall in Chandni Chowk bursts with stories. The graffiti isn't just decoration — it's a dialogue. From playful portraits of shopkeepers to sharp political commentary, the streets are canvases in motion. Meera captures the vibrant chaos of the market where every color screams a different emotion, and every street artist adds a voice to Delhi's never-ending conversation.

Tags: Street Art, Bazaar

The Banyan Oracle

By Kabir | January 2025

In the heart of Lodhi Gardens stands an ancient banyan tree, rumored to grant wisdom to those who listen closely at dawn. Elders say the tree has witnessed centuries of secrets, from royal conspiracies to lovers' promises. Kabir's tale weaves the legend of the Banyan Oracle, where every root and leaf holds a memory of Delhi's past, waiting to be discovered by the patient listener.

Tags: Legend, Nature

Mirza's Lantern

By Sana | December 2024

Every winter, a mysterious lantern glows atop the ruins of Feroz Shah Kotla. Locals whisper that it belongs to Mirza, a forgotten poet who wanders the ramparts searching for his lost verses. Sana's story follows a young boy who, drawn by the light, uncovers a hidden diary and the bittersweet poetry of Mirza, forever illuminating the shadows of Delhi's history.

Tags: Mystery, Poetry
